Call in a Favor · tool used during live work · capability map

Request specific help from your network.

Room type: a tool used while the work is happening · renamed from Advisor Deploy 2026-07-06 · settled (network-assistance workflow)

Call in a Favor links a deal to a person who may help, prepares the request and forwardable note, and records the outcome. Existing relationships do not guarantee influence or buyer cooperation.

Sourced from: canon §4.16 (renamed + deal-first + plain-language teardown, 2026-07-06) + shipped src/advisor-deploy. Settled design: call-in-a-favor-toprail-2026-07-06.html.
1 · Purpose

What the room is for

Keep the request specific, review recent requests to the same person, and record introductions, information, declines, or other actual outcomes. Preserve deal linkage and both message drafts.
2 · The favor as a hand-off

You → your person → the buyer

The room writes both halves. The discipline is making the person’s half easy to say yes to — the easier their lift, the more likely they help, and the less you lean on a favor you may need again.
NoteWhat it is
Your messagewhat you send your person
The forwardable notethe short note they forward to the buyer — they should barely have to change it
3 · The ten asks

What you’re asking for

Ten plain situations, matched to the deal’s state.
  • open the door · get an exec involved · nudge a stalled pilot · unstick procurement · reset the comparison · lost your champion · save it from a budget freeze · signal you’re a serious bet · get a customer to vouch · warm up a renewal
4 · What it offers

The primitives

Capability / surfaceWhat it does
The dealFrom Deal Workspace — the stuck deal the favor moves.
Who to askYour people — investors / advisors / happy customers, each with what they know + which accounts they carry weight with.
What you’re asking forThe ten plain situations (above).
The two notesYour message + the forwardable note.
The ready check“Is this ask small and specific enough to send?”
The don’t-over-ask guardA gentle “second ask in a short window — open with a thank-you” when you’ve asked that person recently.
Deals needing outside helpTop rail, ranked by need, with a count of deals that lack a potential helper.
Log + loop-closeRecord the request outcome on the linked deal, including declines and no response.
5 · Flows

What comes in, what goes out

Flows in
  • deal from Deal Workspace
  • the pilot’s evidence from Pilot Desk
Flows out
  • the favor’s effect on the deal → Deal Workspace · Dashboard · Handoff
6 · Language — plain, no insider jargon

The teardown

The old room was jargon-heavy — all reworded on the face. Code enum keys may stay; the user-facing surface is clean.
Banned on the faceSay instead
“deploy” (people aren’t troops)call in a favor

rolodex / carriersyour people / who’s in your corner
“ask-moment”what you need help with
the economic buyerthe person who signs off
7 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • Link the request and its observed outcome to the specific deal
  • A specific request and a review of recent requests to the same person
  • The hand-off craft (both notes, easy to say yes to)
  • Closing the loop
What it is NOT
  • Not a side log
  • Not a contact CRM
  • Not a mass-intro tool
  • Not a networking rolodex
