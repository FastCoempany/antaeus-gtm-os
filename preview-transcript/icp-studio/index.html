4. What this room is for and how it works

The room's capability map: its purpose, every part and what it does, what flows in from and out to other rooms, and what it must never become.

ICP Studio · Decision Bench · capability map

The one sharp definition of who you sell to.

Decision Bench (strategic shaping) · settled 2026-07-04

Sharpen the one definition of who you sell to so every downstream room can work against a real target. The ICP object is the thing being sharpened — it is the central authored surface, not a form output. Thin means fewer assumptions, fewer personas, fewer use cases. The ICP is the filter that manifests as “ICP Match” scoring on every Account, everywhere.

Sourced from: canon §4.4 + the ICP build + 8-check quality engine (shipped src/icp-studio). Settled design: icp-studio-combo-flow-2026-07-04.html.
1 · Purpose

What the room is for

Make one sharp ICP the filter the whole system runs against. The ICP is a strategic bet the operator authors, not a persona template they fill. Every account, everywhere, carries an ICP-Match score derived from this one definition.
2 · What it offers

Everything the room does

Capability / surfaceWhat it does
The ICP statementThe one authored definition — the central, sharpenable surface.
The buying-group minimumThe smallest real buying group the motion needs.
The focus recommendationThe system’s read of where to sharpen — narrower beats broader.
The shaping inputsowner · pain · trigger · the evidence window (time-to-convince) — the load-bearing fields.
The match histogramHow the current account set scores against this ICP — the quality of the bet made visible.
The 8-check quality engineA 4-tier read on how sharp the ICP is (thinness rewarded, not penalized).
3 · Flows

What comes in, what goes out

Flows in
  • Onboarding activation (the interview-sharpened ICP)
  • founder conviction
Flows out
  • ICP-Match score → Territory Architect · Prospecting Desk · Signal Console · Outbound Studio · Discovery Studio · Readiness · Handoff Kit
4 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • ICP sharpness as a strategic bet
  • Never weaken it into a generic persona form
  • The ICP object is central and authored, not a form output
What it is NOT
  • Not a persona template
  • Not a worksheet
  • Not a demographics form
  • Not a “build your buyer” wizard
