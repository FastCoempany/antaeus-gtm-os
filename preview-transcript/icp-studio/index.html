ICP Studio · strategy and planning · capability map

Define the companies and buyers you sell to.

Type of area: a planning tool for working out a strategic decision before acting on it · settled 2026-07-04

ICP Studio maintains a specific target definition and shows how watched accounts match it. Preserve buyer, problem, trigger, geography, size, and evidence inputs.

Sourced from: canon §4.4 + the ICP build + 8-check quality engine (shipped src/icp-studio). Settled design: icp-studio-combo-flow-2026-07-04.html.
1 · Purpose

What this area is for

Keep the authored ICP central. Explain its specificity using the existing eight checks and account-match evidence. Describe quality as Too broad, Partially defined, Well defined, or Highly specific.
2 · What it offers

Everything ICP Studio does

Capability / surfaceWhat it does
The ICP statementThe one authored definition — the central surface the operator keeps making more specific.
The buying-group minimumThe buyer roles needed for the documented sales process.
The ICP specificity recommendationA recommendation for making the ICP more specific, with the missing inputs identified.
The shaping inputsowner · pain · trigger · the evidence window (time-to-convince) — the fields the quality checks depend on most.
The match histogramHow the current accounts score against this ICP, so you can see how many actually fit.
The 8-check quality engineFour specificity levels derived from eight checks; focused buyer and use-case criteria are preferred.
3 · Flows

What comes in, what goes out

Flows in
  • Onboarding activation (the ICP made specific through the onboarding interview)
  • the founder’s own judgment about who buys
Flows out
  • ICP-Match score → Territory Architect · Prospecting Desk · Signal Console · Outbound Studio · Discovery Studio · Readiness · sales handoff documentation
4 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • ICP specificity as a deliberate targeting decision
  • Never weaken it into a generic persona form
  • The ICP object is central and authored, not a form output
What it is NOT
  • Not a persona template
  • Not a worksheet
  • Not a demographics form
  • Not a “build your buyer” wizard
