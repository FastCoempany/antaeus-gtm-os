Antaeus — the full walkthrough of the preview

This is a written copy of everything a visitor sees in the Antaeus preview: the sample workspace of Elena Vasquez, an enterprise account executive at Luca Industries (a fictional $100MM AI talent platform) selling to real household-name companies. The accounts, deals and history are sample data; every room, read and suggestion is the real product working on that data.

Antaeus is a sales operating system for founders and the first serious sales hire. It turns the work a founder is doing on revenue into a clear picture of what is actually happening, so the first hire can pick it up and run it without having anything re-explained.

How to read this

  1. Start with the gate below — the first screen a visitor sees.
  2. Then go room by room in the order listed. The order follows the sales motion itself, from setting up who you sell to, through finding and working accounts, to advancing deals and handing the motion to a hire.
  3. Each room page has three parts: every word on screen with how and where it is shown; what opens when you use its controls; and the room's purpose, parts and connections.
  4. Each page links to the previous and next room, so you can read the whole app start to finish.

The live app is at /preview on the same site, desktop browsers only.

Onboarding — shown but locked in the preview

Onboarding appears greyed out on the room map. Hovering it shows this:

Onboarding is how a new seller sets up their workspace. They tell the app who they really sell to, name the accounts they're chasing, and say where each of their live deals actually stands — the judgment no tool can fill in for them. The app fills in the rest, and every room you see here wakes up from that one sitting. This preview starts you after that step, on a workspace that's already set up.

What onboarding is and does in the full product:

Onboarding · Threshold · capability map

Do the hard work once. The system plays it back forever.

Threshold (entry) · the heavy-seeding flow · governed by ADR-019 + canon §12

Seed the workspace deep enough that the app “rises up to greet” the operator — every room already live, the Dashboard already handing back a real first move — as a side effect of the operator doing the judgment-heavy work only they can do. Not a low-friction form: a deliberate seeding that asks for the heavy, only-in-your-head thinking and frames it as capitalizing an asset that pays a dividend every morning after.

Sourced from: canon §4.3 + the heavy-seeding doctrine (Part III §12) + ADR-019 (approved, build in progress). Settled design: onboarding-seeding-flow-2026-07-07.html (+ 3 companion validated prototypes).
1 · Purpose

What the room is for

Seed the living rooms (ICP Studio, Signal Console, Deal Workspace, Quota Workback) deep enough that the workspace is already operational when the operator lands on Welcome. The doctrine’s line: we never apologize for the work — we make it obviously worth it, in real time, and tell the operator they’re doing the thing no one else does. They bring irreducible judgment; the app brings enrichment.
2 · The seven-step arc

The settled seeding flow

A fixed, ordered flow. The heavy middle (live deals) is the judgment no automation can supply; everything the app can enrich, it enriches.
#StepWhat it asks / does
1DoorwayLeads with the honesty — “this asks real work of you, here’s the deal.” The honest pitch, not an apology.
2ICPInterview-style — the app pushes back on a vague answer until it’s a target it can hunt.
3AccountsTakes names only (breadth is cheap) and enriches them — triggers, firmographics, heat. They visibly rise up.
4Wake-upThe seed→alive reveal — the system reads the named accounts back, enriched.
5Live dealsThe heavy middle — the operator fills the judgment only they hold (champion, who signs, where it’s stuck); the company came enriched. Deals wake toward the floor.
6QuotaThe number set — seeds Quota Workback + the outbound seed.
7LandingThe system reads everything it was given and hands back the one move that needs the operator first → Welcome.
3 · The tier model

Gate the load-bearing, invite the rest

Heavy-seeding doctrine #6. The deal-level judgment is the hard floor — the thing that stops Antaeus being a prettier CRM.
TierWhatRule
RequiredICP + the account list + ≥10 live dealsGated. Fewer than ten is allowed but the app says plainly it stays thin.
Invitedpilot evidence · advisors · win/loss historyPost-landing, payoff-anchored prompts gathered over days — never walled.

5 · Flows

What comes in, what goes out

Onboarding does not store to a separate “completion” record — its output becomes the live rooms.
Flows in
  • new-user context (company, role, product category)
Flows out
  • gtmos_icp_analytics — the sharpened ICP (→ ICP Studio)
  • gtmos_sc_v4 + gtmos_signal_room_health — accounts (→ Signal Console)
  • gtmos_deal_workspaces — the live deals (→ Deal Workspace)
  • gtmos_qw_inputs + gtmos_outbound_seed — quota (→ Quota Workback)
  • gtmos_activation_context + gtmos_onboarding — completion; all cloud-mirrored via ADR-007. Hands off to Welcome.
6 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • Heavy-seeding — never re-soften into a low-friction form that apologizes for the work
  • Onboarding output becoming the live rooms (never a separate “completion” screen)
  • The ≥10-deal judgment floor
  • The citable evidence margin
  • “They bring judgment, the app brings depth”
What it is NOT
  • Not a low-friction sign-up form
  • Not a CRM data-entry grid
  • Not a wizard that hides the work
  • Not a backstage record the operator never returns to
