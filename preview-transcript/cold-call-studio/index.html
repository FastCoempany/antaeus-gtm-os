Cold Call Studio supports preparation before a call and outcome capture afterward. Preserve the six-step call plan and its buyer-response branches.

Sourced from: canon §4.9 (no-live-call-operation, 2026-07-04) + shipped src/cold-call-studio. Settled design: cold-call-studio-gameplan-2026-07-06.html.
1 · Purpose

What the room is for

Ask about the buyer’s current approach rather than making unsupported claims. Real customer examples may be used only when supplied. Preserve meeting-booked deal creation and the distinctions between callback, voicemail, no answer, referral, and rejection.
2 · The call’s shape

The ordered steps (reviewed beforehand)

Each part carries the pushback a buyer is likely to give, with a line ready for it.
#Step
1Get ready
2Open
3Find the pain
4Show it’s real
5Ask for the meeting
6Wrap up
3 · What it offers

The primitives

Capability / surfaceWhat it does
The call’s shapeThe ordered steps (above), reviewed before the call.
Likely-pushback branchesEach step’s probable brush-off with a ready line.
The get-ready checklistThe pre-flight before the seller walks in.
After-call outcome capturemeeting booked · callback · referral · voicemail · rejected · hung up · no answer.
The after-call summarywhat went well / what to improve next time.
The log of recent callsThe running record of outcomes.
4 · Flows

What comes in, what goes out

Flows in
  • account + what’s happening from Signal Console
  • who to call (the person + their role)
Flows out
  • on a booked meeting → creates a Deal (into Deal Workspace)
  • call outcomes → Dashboard · Deal Workspace · Readiness · sales handoff documentation
5 · Language — plain, mid-sales-legible

The user is the seller

Banned on the face: thread · loom · pull · pressure · proof · say-next · call memory · “the move.” The user is the seller (or you), never “rep.”
6 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • The deal-creation on a booked meeting
  • The prep-then-capture shape
  • The live consequence of a real call
What it is NOT
  • Never a script library
  • Never a during-call clicker
  • Not a live-call console
  • Not a dialer
