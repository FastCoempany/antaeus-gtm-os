4. What this room is for and how it works

The room's capability map: its purpose, every part and what it does, what flows in from and out to other rooms, and what it must never become.

Quota Workback · System Ledger · capability map

The annual number, worked down to what you do every day.

System Ledger (synthesis) · design settled 2026-07-07 (pace + fused strands)

Turn a quota target into execution pressure the seller can feel — the annual number worked all the way down to the one thing they control: what they do every working day. Quota math is execution pressure, not isolated planning.

Sourced from: canon §4.18 + the calc() + computeCoverage engine (shipped src/quota-workback). Settled design: quota-workback-pace-strands-2026-07-07.html.
1 · Purpose

What the room is for

Working backward from the number, answer three things and nothing else: the daily habit the number demands, whether the plan is even real, and whether there’s enough in flight. The daily number is the one dominant state; believability + coverage support it; the funnel arithmetic recedes.
2 · The three answers

What the room resolves

#AnswerWhat it shows
1The daily habitthe outreach/day, meetings/week, real opportunities & deals/quarter the number actually demands
2Is the plan even reala believability check against the benchmark for your deal size — calling out the ONE optimistic assumption + what it costs if you’re wrong (the room’s soul)
3Enough in flightlive pipeline coverage pulled from Deal Workspace — the gap in real dollars
3 · The two reads

Plan (setting) vs Pace (returning)

The room ships in two reads over the same math.
ReadForDominant surface
Plansetting the number — works on an empty workspacethe derived daily habit
Pacea returning sellerprojected year-end landing vs target · what-the-number-needs vs where-you-actually-are · the move back on pace
4 · What it offers

The primitives

Capability / surfaceWhat it does
The annual number (quota)The target the whole room works backward from.
Typical deal size (ACV → benchmark band)SMB / Mid-Market / Enterprise / Strategic — sets which benchmark the believability read tests against.
Win rate + cycle lengthThe conversion + timing inputs.
The conversion assumptionsAdvanced / gated — the funnel arithmetic, recessive.
The derived daily habitmessages & calls a day · first meetings a week · real opportunities & deals a quarter.
The believability read“this plan is a stretch — one assumption is optimistic” + what it costs. A plain judgment, never a bare score.
Pipeline coverageFrom Deal Workspace — ratio vs the band target, weighted vs raw, the dollar gap.
The pace projectionProjected year-end landing vs target + the need-vs-actual scoreboard for a returning seller.
5 · Flows

What comes in, what goes out

Flows in
  • pipeline from Deal Workspace (coverage)
  • logged motion from Outbound / Cold Call (the pace/actuals read)
  • onboarding targets
Flows out
  • targets into the Dashboard
  • coverage truth into Readiness
  • execution pressure (the daily habit) into Outbound + Cold Call
  • the daily/weekly cadence into Founding GTM §7 (day-one operating rhythm)

The shipped face leaned on jargon a first-timer can’t parse — all reworded. Optional room rename flagged for the founder: “Quota Workback” is mild jargon (“workback”); candidates: Your Number / The Daily Number / Backing Into Your Number — not changed yet.

7 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • Quota as execution pressure the seller can feel
  • The daily habit as the one dominant state
  • The believability honesty (the optimistic assumption + its cost) — the room’s soul
  • Pipeline coverage as live reality, not a decorative meter
What it is NOT
  • Not disconnected planning math
  • Not a BI-dashboard card grid
  • Not a decorative meter
  • Not a spreadsheet
