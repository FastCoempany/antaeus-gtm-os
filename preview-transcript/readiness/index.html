Readiness Score · workspace summary · capability map

Could another seller run this sales process?

Room type: a summary that combines evidence from across the workspace into one read · design settled 2026-07-07 (readiness progression)

Readiness assesses operating independence from the recorded evidence. Present the current state, the evidence required for the next state, and practical actions that address missing information.

Sourced from: canon §4.17 + the readiness engine (verdict.ts · dimensions.ts · dashboard readiness-aggregator). Settled design: readiness-score-climb-2026-07-07.html.
1 · Purpose

What the room is for

Keep the five-part gate model and its internal scoring. Display operating states in ordinary language: Founder-dependent, Partially documented, Runnable with founder support, Runnable independently, and Documented and repeatable. A gate is an assessment of available evidence, not a guarantee of sales performance.
2 · The five readiness levels

Gate-based, not threshold-based

Readiness levels are present-evidence gates, not score thresholds. Each gate uses the internal 5-part × 20 math but the face names the state, never a number.
LevelWhat it meansGate (internal)
Founder-dependentFounder-led, no transferable surface yetany part at 0
Partially documentedActivity exists but a hire would still be improvising≥2 parts ≥8, none ≥16
Runnable with founder supportA hire could run it with you there to answer questionsevery part ≥10 AND ≥1 part ≥16 AND first pilot evidence exists
Runnable independentlyAnother seller can run routine work without the founder for two weeksevery part ≥14 AND closed-won exists AND Future Autopsy run AND ≥1 actionable pilot
Documented and repeatableWins and losses recorded, advisors available, and handoff documentation preparedall Runnable independently + win/loss ≥1 AND advisors ≥1 AND handoff ≥5/7 ready
3 · What it offers

Everything the room does

Capability / surfaceWhat it does
The readiness levelThe dominant serif state name + a concise assessment of which work another seller could perform today.
The gating evidenceThe specific present-evidence checks behind the current readiness level.

Readiness progressionThe five levels as a vertical ladder — passed levels sit behind in forest green (“left behind 3 weeks ago”), the current level is “you are here,” the next is the goal + “what it takes.”
The five assessed areasDemoted to quiet readiness labels (forest = solid, amber = limited evidence) — your targeting · your outreach · your discovery · your open deals · your pilot evidence. Never bars, never scores.
Internal dimension scoring5 parts × 20 = 100, kept for the gate math only — never surfaced as a number.

Ceremony hooksTriggers the Founding GTM visual transition on an upward readiness-state transition.
4 · Flows

What comes in, what goes out

Flows in
  • summaries from every deep room (cloud-synced via data-client.ts, not the legacy snapshot key)
  • deal-health vitals from Deal Workspace
  • Pilot Desk outcomes
  • Future Autopsy run history
  • advisor deployment outcomes from Call in a Favor
  • section-readiness count from Founding GTM (drives the repeatable gate)
Flows out
  • readiness level + topbar anchor into the Dashboard
  • a readiness level change UP triggers the visual transition in Founding GTM
  • readiness level + the evidence behind it into the Welcome read

6 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • The readiness level is decided by evidence checks, not a score threshold
  • The central question: can another seller run the process?
  • The visual transition when the readiness level goes UP
  • Readiness progression based on accumulated evidence
What it is NOT
  • Never duplicate what the Dashboard says about “what’s weakest now”
  • Never a 5-bar score breakdown as the primary surface
  • Not a ranking of today’s urgent actions
  • Bars are decoration — the verdict is the value
