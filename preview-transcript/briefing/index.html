4. What this room is for and how it works

The room's capability map: its purpose, every part and what it does, what flows in from and out to other rooms, and what it must never become.

Briefing · Intelligence Surface · capability map

What the system saw — inside your work and out in the market.

Intelligence Surface (new family, closest neighbor System Ledger) · named premium asset · ADR-006

The daily check-in for what the system saw — both inside the operator’s own work (the Workspace stream) and in the market the operator is selling into (the World stream). Written like a peer telling them what they see. Not “what to act on next” (the Dashboard’s job) and not “which accounts are on the radar” (Signal Console’s job).

Sourced from: canon §4.21 + ADR-006 + ADR-009 + ADR-014 + the 13 promoted specs (deliverables/specs/briefing). Settled design: briefing-vivid-2026-07-04.html.
1 · Purpose

What the room is for

Be the daily check-in for what the system saw. The operator’s stated preferences (watchlist, ICP, competitive set) are treated as hypotheses under continuous evaluation, not fixed truths to serve. A responsive product accepts them as given; the Briefing tests them continuously and tells the operator when the evidence isn’t supporting the hypothesis.
2 · The two streams

One room, one toggle

The Workspace / World toggle is the room’s organizing axis, not optional decoration. Default is Workspace.
StreamSubjectCadence · cost
Workspacethe operator’s own deals / signals / pilots (the ADR-009 ledger)daily-fresh · SQL heartbeat generators
Worldthe market — Recipe-Layer Patterns + Contrarian + Peripheryweekly synthesis · full Recipe Layer
3 · The provocative posture

Three obligations the operator did not ask for

The Briefing is provocative, not responsive — it serves stated preferences AND takes on three additional obligations.
ObligationWhat it means
Coveragesurface entities the operator hasn’t named but the data says they should watch (Periphery)
Framingchallenge interpretations the operator has committed to when the evidence contradicts them (Contrarian)
Defensibilitypreserve enough state at every synthesis to reconstruct what the system said, on what basis, months later (Audit Envelopes)

5 · The pipeline

How a Pattern is made

Wrapped end-to-end by the Audit Envelope subsystem; watched by the Evaluation Harness (pre-merge gates + production sampling with retroactive scoring).
  • Context Hydration → Ingest → Filter → Enrich → Periphery Detection → Cluster → Synthesize (Draft → Critique → Revise → Quality Gate) → Contrarian Synthesis → Score → Surface → Briefing Compose
  • Cost discipline: per-user weekly LLM ceiling + separate harness ceiling. Warning at 80%, throttle at 100% (Sonnet substitution + tighter relevance), pause at 150%. Cost telemetry on the footer of every briefing.
6 · Flows

What comes in, what goes out

The Briefing is the intelligence surface that reads from the Signal Console substrate (accounts + signals + heat in Postgres). It never writes to those tables.
Flows in
  • every room’s getState() contract (ICP · Discovery · Outbound · Asset Builder · Deal Workspace · Watchlist Triggers · Voice Document · Behavioral Feedback)
  • the Signal Console substrate’s accounts + signals
  • external sources per the Intelligence Coverage Audit
Flows out
  • recommended_moves[].destination drafts into Discovery Studio · Asset Builder · Outbound · Deal Workspace
  • the Briefing never writes directly — it produces drafts the operator reviews and saves to the destination
7 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • The provocative obligations (Coverage, Framing, Defensibility)
  • The Voice Document’s editorial discipline
  • The audit envelope’s immutability
  • The Workspace / World toggle as the organizing axis
What it is NOT
  • Never a feed
  • Never an inbox
  • Never a notification center
  • Not the Dashboard (“what to act on”) or Signal Console (“who’s on the radar”)
