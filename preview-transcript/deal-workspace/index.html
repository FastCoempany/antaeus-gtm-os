3. What this room is for and how it works

The room's capability map: its purpose, every part and what it does, what flows in from and out to other rooms, and what it must never become.

Deal Workspace · Diagnosis Table · capability map

Which deals will close-lost if you do nothing this week.

Diagnosis Table (intervention) · settled 2026-07-04

Surface the recovery queue — which deals are weakest, why, and what the next corrective move is. The room is an intervention board, not a Kanban. First-fold exposes pressure fast: which deals will close-lost if I do nothing this week, and what’s the smallest corrective move. Stage is not truth unless next-step truth backs it up.

Sourced from: canon §4.13 + the recovery-ranking + 9-field health engine (shipped src/deal-workspace). Settled design: deal-workspace-toggleable-views-2026-07-04.html (Focus / Timeline / List).
1 · Purpose

What the room is for

Lead with pressure. The recovery ranking (staleness + next-step pressure + close-date pressure) sorts every deal into a lane and names the smallest corrective move. Stage truth is only truth when a real dated next step backs it.
2 · The recovery lanes

Where the work is decaying

LaneMeaning
Criticalwill close-lost this week without a move
At-riskdecaying — a corrective move is due
Healthybacked by a real dated next step
3 · The 9-field deal-health modal

What backs the stage

Stage is not truth unless these back it up.
  • champion · economic buyer · use case · pain · competition · decision process · notes · forecast · momentum
4 · What it offers

The primitives

Capability / surfaceWhat it does
The intervention board (dealGrid)The recovery queue — weakest first, each with its cause + smallest corrective move.
The 9-field deal-health modalThe qualification truth behind each deal (above).
The recovery queue panelThe ranked “act on this or lose it” list.
The deal-health panelThe per-deal vitals read.
The loss-reason modalCaptured on transition to lost — feeds Playbook loss patterns.
The toggleable viewsFocus / Timeline / List — different reads on the same pressure.
5 · Flows

What comes in, what goes out

Flows in
  • deals auto-created from Cold Call (on meeting_booked)
  • deal state updates from Discovery Studio
  • pilot state from Pilot Desk
  • advisor effect from Call in a Favor
Flows out
  • deal pressure into Future Autopsy · Dashboard · Readiness · Handoff Kit
  • loss patterns into Founding GTM
6 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • Deal pressure
  • Next-step quality
  • Stage truth (backed by a real next step)
  • Qualification seriousness
What it is NOT
  • Never a passive record page
  • Not a Kanban board
  • Not a pipeline CRM view
  • Not a stage-column drag surface
