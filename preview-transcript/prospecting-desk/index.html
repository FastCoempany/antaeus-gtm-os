Prospecting Desk · strategy and planning · capability map

Find, research, and qualify target accounts.

Room type: a planning tool for working out a strategic decision before acting on it · renamed from Sourcing Workbench 2026-07-03 · settled 2026-07-04 · cited web search added 2026-11-05

The seller describes who they want to sell to, and Antaeus runs its own web search of public news, funding announcements, job posts and company pages. It returns matching companies, each linked to where it was found. The seller can also name companies and get a sourced brief for each. Added companies wait in “Just added” until the seller answers three qualification questions; only then can an account be sent to Signal Console.

1 · Purpose

What the room is for

Turn a plain description of who the seller is after into a short list of real companies with public evidence behind each one, then make the seller check each company before it reaches Signal Console. Research suggests; the seller decides.
2 · The funnel

Find → Qualify → Send

StageWhat happens
Find“Search the web” runs a cited search from the seller’s description (prefilled from the active territory group or the saved ICP). “Research companies you name” returns a sourced brief for up to five named companies. Companies already in Prospecting Desk or watched in Signal Console are left out of search results. The seller adds results one at a time or all at once; they land in “Just added.” Companies can still be added by hand.
Qualifythree qualification questions must be answered by the seller; research can suggest an answer under each question, but a suggestion only fills the field when the seller clicks “Use this”
Sendconfirmed accounts are queued for Signal Console one at a time and appear when that room next loads; Outbound Studio can use a confirmed account for the first message.
3 · The three confirm questions

The qualification step

All three must be answered before an account can be sent to Signal Console. Adding a researched company never answers them and never moves it past “Just added.”
  • Does this account match the target criteria? (target fit)
  • What is the reason or path into the account? (the entry point)
  • How will you reach out? (the outreach approach)
4 · What it offers

The primitives

Capability / surfaceWhat it does
Search the webUp to 8 companies matching the description. Each shows why it fits (one sentence), a dated buying signal with its source link (or “date not given”), who to contact first and why, a possible opening line, a match strength (strong / possible / weak), and its source links.
Research companies you nameFor 1–5 named companies: what they do, up to 5 dated public events with sources, what matches and what could not be confirmed, who to contact first, a possible opening, and suggested answers to the three questions. Names with no public sources are listed plainly.
Citation guardA source counts only if it is an https page the web search actually returned. Companies with no such source are dropped; signals without one are dropped; undated signals are marked undated; duplicates and companies already in the workspace are dropped; every shown sentence passes the voice gate.
Saved searchesSave a description and later “Check for new matches” to re-run it. The result shows only companies not already in the workspace (“N new companies since {date}”), and the saved search records when it was last checked.
Research on the accountAn added company carries its research: “Researched by Antaeus · {date} · N sources,” the signals and source links, what could not be confirmed, and the opening line, shown in the Qualify panel.
Add a company by handEnter a company you found yourself (name, contact, role), optionally linked to a saved search.
Qualification score + what is still missingHow complete an account’s qualification is, and which of the three questions still need an answer.
Who-to-target guidanceThe research’s suggested first contact when there is one; otherwise a standing prompt to prefer the person who owns the problem or the decision related to it.
The stage lifecyclejust added → confirmed → sent, plus set aside (no reason is recorded); counts shown for confirmed and just added.
Cost limitEach workspace has a weekly research budget ($3.00). At 80% the room shows a note; at 100% searches return fewer companies; at 150% search pauses until the next week and the room says so plainly. Every run is recorded in prospecting_research_runs.
Demo laneThe demo has no backend, so it shows sample results labeled “Sample results — the demo doesn’t run a live web search,” built only from companies and facts in the demo data. The only links are the companies’ own websites; sample signals show the publisher name with no link.
5 · Flows

What comes in, what goes out

Flows in
  • Territory Architect — the active territory group prefills the search description and is sent as context
  • ICP Studio — the saved ICP summary is sent as context (and prefills the description when there is no territory group)
  • Signal Console — watched account names are excluded from search results
Flows out
  • confirmed accounts → Signal Console
  • a ready account → Outbound Studio (first message)
  • carries focus + approach context forward
6 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • Honest search — Antaeus runs its own web search and cites every company; never claim a Sales Navigator or data-vendor feed
  • No uncited companies, signals, or links reach the seller
  • Confirm as a real step (accounts pass the three questions before Signal Console); research drafts are suggestions the seller accepts or edits
  • Who-to-target guidance
What it is NOT
  • Never a filing cabinet
  • Not a background watcher — searches run only when the seller clicks (no scheduled re-runs)
  • Not a Sales Navigator integration
  • Not a bought-list importer
  • Not a contact database — it suggests a role to contact, never a named person’s email or phone
