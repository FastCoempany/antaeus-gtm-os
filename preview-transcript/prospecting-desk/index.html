Prospecting Desk · strategy and planning · capability map

Research and qualify target accounts.

Room type: a planning tool for working out a strategic decision before acting on it · renamed from Sourcing Workbench 2026-07-03 · settled 2026-07-04

Prospecting Desk saves target criteria, accepts companies the operator adds, and checks qualification before sending accounts to Signal Console. It does not currently execute a live company search.

Sourced from: canon §4.6 + shipped src/prospecting-desk (v4 surface, verified 2026-11). Canon §4.6 describes Antaeus running its own cited web research; that search is not built — no search service or Edge Function is called from this room. Settled design: prospecting-desk-funnel-2026-07-04.html (variant F1, originally drawn as Find → Confirm → Send; shipped without the automated find step).
1 · Purpose

What the room is for

Keep target criteria, company research, qualification, and the handoff to Signal Console distinct. Saved searches are research criteria the seller keeps, not searches the app runs. If a search service that returns cited companies is built later, update this map then.
2 · The funnel

Save criteria → Qualify → Send

StageWhat happens
Save criteriathe seller saves target criteria in plain words (e.g. “Series-B fintech companies that just raised”), does the research themselves, and adds each company they find. Added companies wait under “Awaiting qualification.”
Qualifythree qualification questions must be answered; only then can the account be sent to Signal Console
Sendconfirmed accounts are queued for Signal Console one at a time and appear when that room next loads; Outbound Studio can use a confirmed account for the first message.
3 · The three confirm questions

The qualification step

All three must be answered before an account can be sent to Signal Console. These are the questions as shipped.
  • Does this account match the target criteria? (target fit)
  • What is the reason or path into the account? (the entry point)
  • How will you reach out? (the outreach approach)
4 · What it offers

The primitives

Capability / surfaceWhat it does
The target-criteria boxSave a plain-language description of the accounts you want. It is stored for reference; it does not run a search.
Saved target criteriaA list of the criteria you saved, each with a count of companies added against it. Nothing is re-run or watched automatically, and no new companies are discovered for you.
Add a companyEnter a company you found (name, contact, role), optionally linked to one of your saved criteria.
Qualification score + what is still missingHow complete an account’s qualification is, and which of the three questions still need an answer.
The three-question confirmThe guard step (above).
Who-to-target guidanceA standing prompt to prefer the person who owns the problem or the decision related to it; the current focus from Territory Architect is shown when one was passed in.
The stage lifecycleawaiting qualification → confirmed → sent, plus set aside (no reason is recorded); counts shown for confirmed and awaiting.
5 · Flows

What comes in, what goes out

Flows in
  • Territory Architect — focus/division, tier, approach vocabulary + who-to-target
Flows out
  • confirmed accounts → Signal Console
  • a ready account → Outbound Studio (first message)
  • carries focus + approach context forward
6 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • Honesty about search — never claim the app searches the web, watches for companies, or has a Sales Navigator or data-vendor feed unless that is actually built
  • Confirm as a real step (accounts pass the three questions before Signal Console)
  • Who-to-target guidance
What it is NOT
  • Never a filing cabinet
  • Never a search box that implies results it cannot produce
  • Not a Sales Navigator integration
  • Not a bought-list importer
