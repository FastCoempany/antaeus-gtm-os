4. What this room is for and how it works

The room's capability map: its purpose, every part and what it does, what flows in from and out to other rooms, and what it must never become.

Outbound Studio · Live Instrument · capability map

One live outbound line, routed before it leaves.

Live Instrument (live execution) · settled 2026-07-04 (conversation-stage spine)

Route one live outbound line — account × buyer × temperature × trigger × next-question — before it leaves. No send path without a named strain: the operator cannot route generic category language into a live channel. Persona × temperature × trigger produces the exact line, and every route keeps a recovery cable on the same board.

Sourced from: canon §4.8 + the send-line generator (5 temperatures × 4 personas · shipped src/outbound-studio). Settled design: outbound-studio-CHOSEN-complete-2026-07-04.html.
1 · Purpose

What the room is for

Turn the operator rack (account · buyer · persona · temperature · trigger · next-question) into the exact spoken/written line — a real, specific outbound move, never template spam. The generator maps 5 temperatures × 4 personas to a send line + a quality read + a motion band.

The primitives

Quality + motion bandready ≥80 / workable ≥60 / thin — is the line real enough to send.

4 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • Motion craft + anti-spam seriousness
  • No send path without a named strain
  • The recovery cable on every route
What it is NOT
  • Never a template-spam generator
  • Not a mail-merge tool
  • Not a sequence blaster
  • Not a generic copy generator
