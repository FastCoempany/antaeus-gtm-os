4. What this room is for and how it works

The room's capability map: its purpose, every part and what it does, what flows in from and out to other rooms, and what it must never become.

Dashboard · Command Chamber · capability map

The one ranked object under the most pressure — and the one move.

Command Chamber (ranking) · settled 2026-07-04

Show the operator the single ranked object under the most pressure right now, with one dominant next move, without browsing. The Dashboard is where the operator stops choosing between modules and starts being told which deal, signal, or pilot to act on first — it ranks everything under pressure, explains why a specific object came up first, and offers one compressed act-or-inspect move.

Sourced from: canon §4.2 + the command-intelligence engine (Part V · buildCommandObjects / rankCommandObjects / summarizeCommandContext / explainCommandObject). Settled design: dashboard-CHOSEN-complete-2026-07-04.html.
1 · Purpose

What the room is for

Replace the hallway (equal-weight doors) with a smarter front door: one ranked object, one dominant move, the reasoning shown. Three density modes support different cognitive states; all three keep the ranking. This is the room the whole system’s workspace-health summaries flow into. Supersession (2026-07-08, founder-confirmed): the settled 2026-07-04 "command + standing cockpit" folds the three cognitive states into one surface (standing = the Brief read · the one move = Spotlight · skip-the-board = Queue), so there is no explicit mode switcher, and the "this week's reads" card is not carried on the Dashboard. Sections 2 + the "this week's reads" row below are retained as history.
2 · The three density modes

Brief / Spotlight / Queue — same ranking, different cognitive state — superseded 2026-07-08, folded into the cockpit

ModeShapeFor the operator who…
BriefA 2–5 sentence narrative read + one variable-insight linewants the morning read in prose, then the one move
SpotlightThe single focal object at depth + a ranked “next” railwants to act on the top object and see why it’s first
QueueA ranked triage list — rank prefix + reason chips per rowwants to work down the pressure list one at a time

4 · The ranking engine

How the order is decided

The command-intelligence engine builds command objects from every room’s snapshot, ranks them, and explains the order. Family priority breaks ties.
FamilyPrioritySource
risk5deal-room pressure (top_pressure entries)
advisor / opportunity4advisor asks · signal/outbound moves

icp2fires when accounts/signals/deals exist but no ICP
system1dependency warnings the system raises
5 · Flows

What comes in, what goes out

Flows in
  • workspace-health summaries from every deep room
  • the system-health variant from Readiness + Quota Workback
  • signal heat from Signal Console (gtmos_signal_room_health)
  • deal pressure from Deal Workspace (gtmos_deal_workspace_health)
Flows out
  • one-click routing into whichever room owns the ranked object — with focusObject + returnTo preserved
  • the Readiness drawer overlay (verdict + gating evidence)
6 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • The whole-motion standing leads the room (the verdict masthead) · one dominant move under it · the standing row where each part is a door (the cockpit; supersedes the Brief/Spotlight/Queue modes, 2026-07-08)
  • The room-browsing rail does not belong center stage
  • The ranking must keep showing its reasoning — never reduced to decorative scores
  • One dominant move per surface
What it is NOT
  • Not a dashboard of vanity metrics
  • Not a grid of equal-weight boxes
  • Not a hallway of module doors
  • Not a BI report
