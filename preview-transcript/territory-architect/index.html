Territory Architect · strategy and planning · capability map

Define and prioritize territory segments.

Room type: a planning tool for working out a strategic decision before acting on it · settled 2026-07-04 (axis-morphing floor)

Territory Architect organizes target accounts into segments with priority tiers, approaches, and a 300-account ceiling. Segments can combine geography, industry, size, named accounts, and observable buying signals.

Sourced from: canon §4.5 (carve-axis malleability + why-you/why-now charter, 2026-07-03) + shipped src/territory-architect. Settled design: territory-architect-axis-morph-2026-07-04.html.
1 · Purpose

What the room is for

Define the account group, its priority tier, and why it fits. Require a current reason for signal-based segments. Preserve account allocation, reassignment, and the existing stable focus and axis identifiers.
2 · The segmentation criteria

How a segment is cut — blendable

However a segment is defined, the same machinery sits on top: a tier, a share of the 300-cap, an approach, and a charter. The room morphs its register to the axis.
AxisReads like
Geographycartographic
Industry / verticalan industry board
Segment (size / ACV / stage)a segment grid
Named accountsa named list
Trigger / behaviordocumented buying signals
Blended (e.g. West Coast × FinTech × Series B+)a coverage funnel keeping the blend legible
3 · The charter rule

Every segment states why

The charter adapts its ask to the axis so the operator never reads the rule.
FieldRule
Why-you (why we’re the right team)required — every segment, every axis
Why-now (the current event or business need)required for trigger segments · optional-but-encouraged for structural segments
Signalsmust be observable (a fresh raise, a new exec, a hiring surge) — never a trigger no one can detect
4 · What it offers

The primitives

Capability / surfaceWhat it does

Focus-to-account taggingWhich accounts belong to which segment.
The 300-cap allocationcount-vs-target per tier + a swap mechanic — each account is consequential.
5 · Flows

What comes in, what goes out

Flows in
  • ICP Studio (the specific target definition)
Flows out

  • tier + focus into Signal Console
6 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • The strategic tier logic
  • Define each target-account segment
  • Axis-malleability (never hardcode one segmentation)
  • The why-you charter on every segment
  • Divisions standing on detectable signals
What it is NOT
  • Never a contact-list builder
  • Not a static map
  • Not a ranked leaderboard of accounts
  • Not a single-axis (geo-only) territory tool
