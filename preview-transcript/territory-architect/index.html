4. What this room is for and how it works

The room's capability map: its purpose, every part and what it does, what flows in from and out to other rooms, and what it must never become.

Territory Architect · Decision Bench · capability map

The ICP, carved into a territory you actually own.

Decision Bench (strategic shaping) · settled 2026-07-04 (axis-morphing floor)

Turn the ICP into a tiered territory with focuses, approaches, and a hard 300-account ceiling that forces strategic ownership. The territory is a map of focused groups of buyers, not a list. A focus is a named division of the market, carved on whatever axis fits the motion — and the axes blend.

Sourced from: canon §4.5 (carve-axis malleability + why-you/why-now charter, 2026-07-03) + shipped src/territory-architect. Settled design: territory-architect-axis-morph-2026-07-04.html.
1 · Purpose

What the room is for

Carve the ICP into divisions the seller can actually own. The carve axis is a variable, not a constant — the room stays malleable from a scorch-the-earth pre-seed (one coarse division) to an organized Series B (a geo × vertical grid). The 300-cap + swap make each account consequential; tiers are a resource-allocation commitment, not a ranking.
2 · The carve axes

How a division is cut — blendable

However a division is carved, the same machinery sits on top: a tier, a share of the 300-cap, an approach, and a charter. The room morphs its register to the axis.
AxisReads like
Geographycartographic
Industry / verticalan industry board
Segment (size / ACV / stage)a segment grid
Named accountsa named list
Trigger / behaviorpressure charters
Blended (e.g. West Coast × FinTech × Series B+)a coverage funnel keeping the blend legible
3 · The charter rule

Every division states why

The charter adapts its ask to the axis so the operator never reads the rule.
FieldRule
Why-you (why we’re the right team)required — every division, every axis
Why-now (what pressure is on them)required for trigger carves · optional-but-encouraged for structural carves
Signalsmust be observable (a fresh raise, a new exec, a hiring surge) — never a trigger no one can detect
4 · What it offers

The primitives

Capability / surfaceWhat it does
Sales-cycle calibrationTunes the territory to the motion’s cycle length.
The carve axisgeo / vertical / segment / named / trigger — blendable; sets the room’s register.
Focuses / divisionsThe named cuts of the market.
Per-division charterwhy-you (required) · why-now (conditional on axis).
Approaches + the approach ledgerHow each division gets worked, tracked.
TiersA resource-allocation commitment, not a ranking.
Focus-to-account taggingWhich accounts belong to which division.
The 300-cap allocationcount-vs-target per tier + a swap mechanic — each account is consequential.
5 · Flows

What comes in, what goes out

Flows in
  • ICP Studio (the sharpened target)
Flows out
  • tier + focus into Prospecting Desk
  • tier + focus into Signal Console
6 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • The strategic tier logic
  • The act of naming a division
  • Axis-malleability (never hardcode one carve)
  • The why-you charter on every division
  • Divisions standing on detectable signals
What it is NOT
  • Never a contact-list builder
  • Not a static map
  • Not a ranked leaderboard of accounts
  • Not a single-axis (geo-only) territory tool
