3. What this room is for and how it works

The room's capability map: its purpose, every part and what it does, what flows in from and out to other rooms, and what it must never become.

Signal Console · Live Instrument · capability map

The accounts you’re watching, the signals stacking up, the next move on each.

Live Instrument (live execution) · protected room · settled 2026-07-04 (Attention Router)

The room where the operator tracks the named accounts they’re watching, the signals attached to each, and the next move on each. Accounts come in by hand or from enrichment; signals stack up against them; the operator decides what to act on. Signals are time-limited events — research on a qualified account may justify motion, but research without motion is collection theater.

Sourced from: canon §4.7 + the heat engine (shipped src/signal-console) + ADR-006 (substrate role). Settled design: signal-console-attention-router-2026-07-04.html.
1 · Purpose

What the room is for

Rank the watched field by where attention should go. Account heat feeds Dashboard command ranking. Post-ADR-006 the room is also the data substrate the Briefing reads from — accounts + signals live in the signal_console_accounts + signals Postgres tables this room owns. The Briefing reads; it never writes back.
2 · The heat engine

How an account ranks

Heat is computed, not stored — the source of truth is the signals + their timestamps.
  • Heat = signal count × type weight × source credibility × recency decay
  • Bands (the Attention Router): act now · reach while warm · emerging · going cold — the relevance is felt as a band, not a bare number

4 · Flows

What comes in, what goes out

Flows in
  • accounts from Prospecting Desk + Territory Architect
  • ICP-Match score from ICP Studio
Flows out
  • ranked accounts → Outbound Studio · Cold Call Studio · LinkedIn Playbook
  • heat + readiness → Dashboard
  • heat + motion state → Handoff Kit
  • accounts + signals → the Briefing (read-only substrate per ADR-006)
  • act-on handoffs → Outbound · Discovery Studio · Cold Call · Deal Workspace
5 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • Signal interpretation
  • Account-to-motion logic (research without motion is collection theater)
  • Account priority meaning
  • A protected room — may not be made generic, renamed, or folded in
What it is NOT
  • Never a badge list
  • Never a passive timeline
  • Not a notification feed
  • Not a static account directory
