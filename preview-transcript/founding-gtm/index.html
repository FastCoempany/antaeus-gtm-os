4. What this room is for and how it works

The room's capability map: its purpose, every part and what it does, what flows in from and out to other rooms, and what it must never become.

Founding GTM · System Ledger · capability map

The document a hire opens on day one.

System Ledger (synthesis) · design settled 2026-07-07 (the open book)

The living onboarding surface the first hire opens on day one — where the founder’s tacit GTM memory becomes a transferable artifact. Not an export; the workspace itself is the durable artifact post cloud-sync. The kit’s job is authored opinion + cross-room synthesis, not aggregation — it renders the founder’s hard-won understanding as prescriptive, surprising, specific content the hire can read top-to-bottom.

Sourced from: canon §4.19 + the section authoring engines (src/founding-gtm/lib/sections.ts + cross-room.ts). Settled design: founding-gtm-open-book-2026-07-07.html.
1 · Purpose

What the room is for

Do what the workspace cannot: render the founder’s GTM understanding as prescriptive, surprising, specific content. Specificity comes from authored opinion (not bullet aggregation) and from cross-room reads no single room could surface alone. This is the culmination — every other room points toward getting the workspace ready to hand off.
2 · The seven sections

Each authored, each with a cross-room “one thing to notice”

Replacing the legacy six. Each section is authored prose + a concrete evidence line + one cross-room read (the “surprise” primitive in code; renders as a plain “one thing to notice” margin note, never labeled “SURPRISE”).

4 · The ceremony moment

The kit becomes real

When Readiness transitions UP from Building → Inheritable with guardrails for the first time in a workspace, fire a set-piece (not a toast): an animated reveal — the verdict change, the kit count moving (e.g. 2/7 → 4/7), a serif headline (“the kit just became real”), and a one-time CTA to share the workspace read-mode. Once per workspace per upward transition; downward transitions are silent.
5 · Flows

What comes in, what goes out

Flows in
  • every cloud-synced room — ICP Studio (§1) · Deal Workspace closed deals (§1/4/5/6) · Outbound + LinkedIn + Cold Call (§2) · Discovery segment-jump data (§3) · Call in a Favor (§4) · Future Autopsy (§5) · Quota Workback + Deal pipeline (§7)
Flows out
  • section-readiness count into the Dashboard + Readiness anchor (the “5/7 ready” stat)
  • section-readiness count drives the Readiness “Hire-ready, repeatable” gate (≥ 5/7)
6 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • The authored-opinion frame (reads like a document a sharp operator wrote for their replacement)
  • The cross-room “one thing to notice” reads
  • The ceremony moment
  • Section 7’s prescriptive cadence is the operating rhythm, not metrics
What it is NOT
  • Never an export of room data
  • Never a bullet-list aggregator (the legacy room was that; the rebuild explicitly is not)
  • Never a dashboard of section-cards
  • Never just a scoreboard
