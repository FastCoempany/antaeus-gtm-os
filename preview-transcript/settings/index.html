3. What this room is for and how it works

The room's capability map: its purpose, every part and what it does, what flows in from and out to other rooms, and what it must never become.

Settings · Trust Annex · capability map

Your workspace is safe. Here’s how to keep it that way.

Trust Annex (utility) · settled 2026-07-07 (the safe deposit)

Keep the user safe, make trust and recovery real, without stealing attention from live operating rooms. Open with a plain reassurance — “your workspace is safe, it’s saved to the cloud as you work, not trapped on this laptop” — then the controls to take a copy, restore, or erase it, then the quiet preferences and account controls. Calm, plainspoken utility with a hierarchy: data safety leads; preferences and account recede.

Sourced from: canon §4.20 (the safe-deposit redesign + language teardown, 2026-07-07) + shipped src/settings. Settled design: settings-safe-deposit-2026-07-07.html.
1 · Purpose

What the room is for

Make trust + recovery real without operating-room energy. The redesign fixed a real drift (the pre-arc 9-card grid): the dual-export confusion is resolved, account controls were added, and demo mode is tucked. Data safety is the point of the room, so it leads.

5 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • The trust signals (the plain “your workspace is safe” reassurance, the honest delete)
  • Data-safety as the room’s dominant billing (never buried among preferences)
  • The account controls (sign out, privacy/terms)
What it is NOT
  • Never mixed with operating-room energy
  • Never internal-architecture language as copy
  • Not a 9-card grab-bag
  • Not a fake-excitement product surface
