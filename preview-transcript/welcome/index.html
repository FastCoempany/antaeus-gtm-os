Welcome summarizes the configured workspace, the highest-priority recommended action, and recent changes. The recommendation and its evidence come from the Dashboard ranking engine.

Sourced from: canon §4.1 + the heavy-seeding doctrine (§12) + the seeding flow (§4.3). Settled design: welcome-flow-landing-2026-07-07.html.
1 · Purpose

What the room is for

After onboarding and on later visits, show the current setup state, the recommended action with supporting evidence, and account/deal totals. Keep one primary action and a secondary route to Dashboard. Do not claim that analysis happened overnight unless that execution is recorded.
2 · What it offers

Everything the room does

Capability / surfaceWhat it does
The setup-state headingA prominent heading names setup completion or welcomes a returning user. Supporting information reflects the available account and deal evidence.
The highest-priority recommended actionThe highest-ranked current recommendation, its evidence, and one action link. Do not imply an overnight execution that was not recorded.
Recent changesTwo to four concise lines describing recent account and deal changes, with their supporting inputs.
The operating lineA secondary summary of watched accounts, active deals, and open pipeline.
The calm route to the DashboardA quiet, non-competing path to the full command surface for the operator who wants more than the recommended action.
3 · The two lifecycles

One layout, two headlines

After setup and re-entry reuse the exact same shape; only the headline changes. Both lifecycle states use the same overview layout.
LifecycleHeadline registerWhat the overview says
After setup“Workspace setup is complete.”the recommended action and available setup data
Re-entry“Changes since your last visit.”account/deal movement since last session + the recommended action
4 · Flows

What comes in, what goes out

Flows in
  • gtmos_activation_context + the full seeded workspace from Onboarding (§4.3)
  • the ranked recommendation from the Dashboard command-intelligence engine
  • account + deal movement since the last session
Flows out
  • directional handoff into the room that owns the recommended action — Signal Console / Deal Workspace / Outbound
  • the calm route to the full Dashboard
5 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • Welcome as the onboarding data collection’s landing — never an empty-workspace “create your first…” threshold
  • one primary recommended action (never two competing)
  • The setup results made visible (“changes since your last visit”)
  • never gamified, no finish line
What it is NOT
  • Not an empty on-ramp or a setup wizard
  • Not a dashboard of vanity counts
  • Not a gamified progress ladder
  • Not a “all done” celebration screen
