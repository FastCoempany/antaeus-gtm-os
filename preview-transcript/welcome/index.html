3. What this room is for and how it works

The room's capability map: its purpose, every part and what it does, what flows in from and out to other rooms, and what it must never become.

Welcome · Threshold · capability map

The first morning the setup pays back.

Threshold (entry) · the seeding flow’s landing · design settled 2026-07-07

The threshold the operator hits on entry — the moment the app “rises up to greet” them and hands back the one move. Welcome is where the seeding flow lands: after setup the workspace is already deep (ICP + accounts + ≥10 deals + quota), so this is never an empty on-ramp — it is the first morning the hard work reads itself back.

Sourced from: canon §4.1 + the heavy-seeding doctrine (§12) + the seeding flow (§4.3). Settled design: welcome-flow-landing-2026-07-07.html.
1 · Purpose

What the room is for

Close the loop the onboarding doorway opened — “do the hard work once, the system reads your motion back to you every morning” — and make that playback the whole room. One commanding statement, one dominant move (the system’s top pick, read overnight across the entire workspace), and the payback shown as a quiet secondary read. Progress is felt as becoming operational, never gamified, no finish line.
2 · What it offers

Everything the room does

Capability / surfaceWhat it does
The commanding statementOne serif line that names the state — day-one landing (“the workspace is awake”) vs re-entry (“what moved since you left”). The only thing that changes between the two lifecycles.
The one dominant moveThe system’s single overnight pick + its plain reason + one CTA. The system read the whole workspace while the operator was away; this is the one thing that needs them first.
The “what the system saw” read2–4 plain lines — the dividend on the setup work. Never a checklist, never a status dump. What the system noticed on its own.
The operating lineA recessive read: accounts watched · live deals · in flight. The “morning door” into the workspace — never “all done.”
The calm route to the DashboardA quiet, non-competing path to the full command surface for the operator who wants more than the one move.
3 · The two lifecycles

One layout, two headlines

Day-one landing and re-entry reuse the exact same shape; only the headline changes. This is the whole trick of the room — the payback surface is identical whether it’s the first morning or the hundredth.
LifecycleHeadline registerWhat the read says
Day-one landing“The workspace is awake — here’s what you just switched on.”the first move + what the seeding produced
Re-entry“What moved since you left.”account/deal movement since last session + the one move
4 · Flows

What comes in, what goes out

Flows in
  • gtmos_activation_context + the full seeded workspace from Onboarding (§4.3)
  • the system’s ranked pick from the Dashboard command-intelligence engine
  • account + deal movement since the last session
Flows out
  • directional handoff into the room that owns the one move — Signal Console / Deal Workspace / Outbound
  • the calm route to the full Dashboard
5 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • Welcome as the seeding flow’s landing — never an empty-workspace “create your first…” threshold
  • one dominant move (never two competing)
  • the payback made visible (“what the system saw while you were away”)
  • never gamified, no finish line
What it is NOT
  • Not an empty on-ramp or a setup wizard
  • Not a dashboard of vanity counts
  • Not a gamified progress ladder
  • Not a “all done” celebration screen
