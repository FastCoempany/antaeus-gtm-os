How it works

  1. 1

    Tell us the idea.

    Three sentences, who uses it, the screens you want to see.

  2. 2

    We build it.

    Three to five screens, one flow, fake data, in your colors if you have them.

  3. 3

    You get a link.

    Click through it, send it to whoever needs to see it. One round of changes included.
