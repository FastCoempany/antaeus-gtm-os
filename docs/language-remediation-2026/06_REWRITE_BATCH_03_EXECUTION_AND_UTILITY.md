# Rewrite Batch 03 — Execution, Deal Management, Quota, Events, and Settings

This batch is intentionally conservative. Standard sales terminology is preserved. The purpose is to remove remaining private ontology, personification, aphoristic framing, and causal overstatement around otherwise good operational language.

- Decision process
- Notes
- Messages and calls
- First meetings
- Pipeline
- Quota / target
- Closed won / closed lost
- Pilot success criteria
- Legal / Security / Finance / Business approval

These are familiar sales/business nouns and should not be replaced merely to create Antaeus-specific language.

## Causal-language rule

Do not say an outreach line, discovery question, event, or behavior `won`, `caused`, `made`, or `proved` an outcome unless the underlying system can establish causality. Prefer observational language such as `appeared in`, `was used on`, `was associated with`, or `occurred before`.

## Generated message rule

The cold-call, outbound, LinkedIn, and discovery content can remain conversational. The ban is not on natural speech. The ban is on private Antaeus ontology and rhetorical compression that obscures meaning.
