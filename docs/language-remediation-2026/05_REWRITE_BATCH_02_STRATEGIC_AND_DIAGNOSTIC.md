## Structural requirements by room

### Territory Architect

The user should create **territory segments**, not `carve divisions`. Segment criteria are geography, industry, company size/stage, named accounts, buying signals, or combined criteria. Every segment should have a plain name, priority tier, fit rationale, and optional current reason to prioritize it.

### Signal Console

Retire the temperature model as the primary information architecture. The surface should show **account priority**, the signals/activity that drive that priority, and the age of those signals. The existing numeric score can remain only if it is explicitly named and explainable.

Recommended bands:
- High priority
- Priority declining
- New signals
- Monitor / low recent activity

Do not use `warming`, `hot`, `cool`, `cold`, `ripe`, or `heat building` to carry functional meaning.

### Briefing

Organize the room around:
1. Changes in your deals and accounts
2. Market patterns
3. Evidence that conflicts with a workspace assumption
4. Accounts to consider adding
5. Saved monitoring rules

Every analysis should expose **observation → evidence → interpretation → recommended action**. Remove `read` as the generic noun for analysis and remove system-personification language.

### Getting to Signed

Use four standard workstreams:
- Legal
- Security
- Finance
- Business approval

For each show: customer request, your proposed position, gap, status, owner, next action, and due date. `Front`, `face-off`, `fight`, `your line`, and `run-to-signed` are retired.

### Pilot Desk

Use standard pilot language: success criteria, approver, end date, participants, engagement, adoption, results summary, next decision. Do not use `finish line`, `hold their hand`, or `carry` as the primary explanation.

### Founding GTM

Treat this as **sales handoff documentation**, not an authored aphoristic document. Avoid causal claims the data cannot prove. Replace `questions that won the next meeting` with observational language such as `questions used on calls that progressed`.

### Call in a Favor

Keep the room name if desired; it is understandable. Replace the internal `relay/backchannel/spend trust` conceptual system with standard network-assistance language. Track who can help, what is being asked, the forwardable note, recent request history, and outcome.
