A COMMON HIRING MISTAKE

A revenue gap doesn’t mean you need another salesperson.

A company can be short of revenue and still have enough sellers. The real limit may be too little pipeline, a low win rate, too few accounts to sell into, deals that only close when the founder sells them, the time a new hire needs to ramp, or no one with time to manage them.

“We need another $1.2M.”

≠

“We need another AE.”

The analysis looks at the revenue gap and the hiring question separately, so you can see which problem you actually have.

WHAT WE CHECK

Another AE is supported only if four things hold up in the numbers.

  1. 01 — Revenue need

    Is additional selling capacity actually required?

    We compare your revenue plan with what your current sellers can realistically close.

  2. 02 — Pipeline

    Is there enough qualified pipeline for another seller?

    We check whether there is enough existing pipeline to hand the new seller, and whether you create new opportunities fast enough to keep them busy.

  3. 03 — Repeatability

    Can someone other than the founder close these deals?

    We separate the deals the founder closed from the deals other sellers closed, and check whether there are enough of the second kind to plan on.

  4. 04 — Timing

    Can the hire contribute when the plan needs the revenue?

    We add up the time it takes to recruit, ramp and close a first deal, and check that someone has time to manage the new hire.

THE MATH

We start with the revenue you need and work backward to the pipeline one more seller would require.

67 × $75,000

That is only the first step. We then check whether your current team has room for more deals, whether that pipeline can actually be handed to a new seller, whether your win rate holds up when the founder isn’t selling, and whether the revenue can close within the period your plan needs it.

The question isn’t whether you want another AE. It’s whether your business can give that AE the pipeline and support they would need to reach their quota.

QUESTIONS

Questions before you buy

Is this evaluating the candidate?

No. It checks whether your business and sales process can support adding the role right now. Whether a particular candidate is good is a separate question.

What if I do not know every number?

Answer I don’t know. The brief lists that number under what we couldn’t confirm. We don’t quietly fill it in with an industry average.

Do you need CRM access?

No. The analysis uses only the answers you give in the questionnaire.

What do I receive?

A written AE Hiring Brief with the answer, the seller-capacity and pipeline math, the timing, the assumption that would change the answer most, what we couldn’t confirm, and what has to be true before you hire.

What happens if the answer is “not yet”?

The brief names what is holding the hire back and what would have to change before the hire is supported.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is an analysis of your sales numbers and the assumptions behind them. The decision to hire, and any financial decision, stays with you.

Is this based on industry benchmarks?

No, not by default. The analysis uses your company’s own numbers first. If an outside figure is ever used, the brief labels it as an outside assumption so it isn’t mistaken for your own data.

How is our company information handled?

This page does not collect any company information. The intake, and the policy for how your answers are stored, accessed and deleted, will be published before checkout opens. The general Antaeus privacy policy covers this website.
