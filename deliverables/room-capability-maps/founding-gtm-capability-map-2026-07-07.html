Founding GTM · workspace summary · capability map

Document the sales process for another seller.

Room type: a summary that combines evidence from across the workspace into one read · design settled 2026-07-07 (the open book)

Founding GTM synthesizes account, outreach, discovery, deal, pilot, and outcome records into sales handoff documentation. Keep samples and evidence beside conclusions.

Sourced from: canon §4.19 + the section authoring engines (src/founding-gtm/lib/sections.ts + cross-room.ts). Settled design: founding-gtm-open-book-2026-07-07.html.
1 · Purpose

What the room is for

Report observed patterns, sample sizes, and limitations. Associations do not establish that an outreach message, question, or action caused a win. Recommend what to test next while preserving the seven sections, observations that combine evidence from several rooms, and sharing workflow.
2 · The seven sections

Each written as prose, each with one key observation drawn from several rooms

Replacing the legacy six. Each section is authored prose + a concrete evidence line + one observation that combines evidence from several rooms in this workspace (the “surprise” field in code; shown on the page as a “Key observation” note, never labeled “SURPRISE”).

4 · The ceremony moment

Sales handoff readiness improves

When Readiness transitions UP from Partially documented → Usable with founder support for the first time in a workspace, show the existing visual transition (not a toast): an animated reveal — the readiness level changing, the kit count moving (e.g. 2/7 → 4/7), a serif headline announcing the milestone, and a button to open the handoff document. (The canon also calls for a one-time prompt to share the workspace read-only; the shipped overlay has only the open button — sharing stays in the room’s share control.) Once per workspace per upward transition; downward transitions are silent.
5 · Flows

What comes in, what goes out

Flows in
  • every cloud-synced room — ICP Studio (§1) · Deal Workspace closed deals (§1/4/5/6) · Outbound + LinkedIn + Cold Call (§2) · Discovery segment-jump data (§3) · Call in a Favor (§4) · Future Autopsy (§5) · Quota Workback + Deal pipeline (§7)
Flows out
  • section-readiness count into the Dashboard + Readiness anchor (the “5/7 ready” stat)
  • section-readiness count drives the Readiness “Hire-ready and repeatable” gate (≥ 5/7)
6 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • Specific recommendations to the seller, supported by recorded evidence
  • The key observations that combine evidence from several rooms
  • The ceremony moment
  • Section 7’s prescriptive cadence is the operating rhythm, not metrics
What it is NOT
  • Never an export of room data
  • Never a bullet-list aggregator (the legacy room was that; the rebuild explicitly is not)
  • Never a dashboard of section-cards
  • Never just a scoreboard
