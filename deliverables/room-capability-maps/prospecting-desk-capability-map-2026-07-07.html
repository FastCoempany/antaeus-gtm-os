Find accounts, confirm each one, send only the real ones on.

Decision Bench (strategic shaping) · renamed from Sourcing Workbench 2026-07-03 · settled 2026-07-04

Find accounts, confirm each one is real and worth it, and send only the confirmed ones to Signal Console. Keep the funnel moving — never a filing cabinet. Antaeus runs its own web research: the operator describes who they want in plain language, and Antaeus reads public news, funding announcements, job posts and company pages, and brings back companies that match — each cited with a link.

1 · Purpose

What the room is for

Run one top-to-bottom funnel — Find → Confirm → Send. The room does not plug into Sales Navigator or buy contact lists; it runs its own cited web research. Confirm is a real, visible step — the discipline that guards Signal Console. Nothing crosses without it.
2 · The funnel

Find → Confirm → Send

StageWhat happens
Findthe operator describes who they want → Antaeus runs its own web research → cited companies land in “Just added.”
Confirmthree plain questions turn a raw company into a qualified account — the guard on Signal Console
Sendconfirmed accounts cross to Signal Console; a ready account can also go to Outbound (first message). Each sends individually.
3 · The three confirm questions

The real, visible confirm step

Plain-language, spoon-fed — nothing insider.
  • Are they who we’re targeting? (does the account fit the focus)
  • What’s our way in to the account? (the entry point)
  • How will we reach out? (the approach)

What comes in, what goes out

  • Territory Architect — focus/division, tier, approach vocabulary + who-to-target
Flows out

6 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • The honesty of the search — Antaeus runs its OWN web research, cited (never claim a vendor/Sales-Nav feed it doesn’t have)
  • Confirm as a real step (accounts pass the three questions before Signal Console)
  • Who-to-target guidance
What it is NOT
  • Never a filing cabinet
  • Never a “pretend search”
  • Not a Sales Navigator integration
  • Not a bought-list importer
