The one ranked object under the most pressure — and the one move.

Command Chamber (ranking) · settled 2026-07-04

Show the operator the single ranked object under the most pressure right now, with one dominant next move, without browsing. The Dashboard is where the operator stops choosing between modules and starts being told which deal, signal, or pilot to act on first — it ranks everything under pressure, explains why a specific object came up first, and offers one compressed act-or-inspect move.

1 · Purpose

What the room is for

Replace the hallway (equal-weight doors) with a smarter front door: one ranked object, one dominant move, the reasoning shown. Three density modes support different cognitive states; all three keep the ranking. This is the room the whole system’s workspace-health summaries flow into. Supersession (2026-07-08, founder-confirmed): the settled 2026-07-04 "command + standing cockpit" folds the three cognitive states into one surface (standing = the Brief read · the one move = Spotlight · skip-the-board = Queue), so there is no explicit mode switcher, and the "this week's reads" card is not carried on the Dashboard. Sections 2 + the "this week's reads" row below are retained as history.

6 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • The whole-motion standing leads the room (the verdict masthead) · one dominant move under it · the standing row where each part is a door (the cockpit; supersedes the Brief/Spotlight/Queue modes, 2026-07-08)
  • The room-browsing rail does not belong center stage
  • The ranking must keep showing its reasoning — never reduced to decorative scores
  • One dominant move per surface
What it is NOT
  • Not a dashboard of vanity metrics
  • Not a grid of equal-weight boxes
  • Not a hallway of module doors
  • Not a BI report
