Cold Call Studio · Live Instrument · capability map

Walk into the cold call ready. Log what happened after.

Live Instrument (live execution) · settled 2026-07-06 (the game plan · prep-before / log-after)

Help the seller walk into a cold call ready — the opener, the pain to find, how to show it’s real, the ask, and the pushback they’re likely to hit — and capture what happened after. It prepares the call and logs the result; it does not ask the seller to operate the app during the call. A cold call is wildly unpredictable — the seller can’t follow a clock or click buttons mid-call.

Sourced from: canon §4.9 (no-live-call-operation, 2026-07-04) + shipped src/cold-call-studio. Settled design: cold-call-studio-gameplan-2026-07-06.html.
1 · Purpose

What the room is for

A prep surface + an after-the-call capture surface, never a during-call console. The seller reviews the shape and the likely branches beforehand so they walk in with conviction, makes the call, and logs the outcome after. Won by walking in prepared, not by reading a screen mid-call.
2 · The call’s shape

The ordered steps (reviewed beforehand)

Each part carries the pushback a buyer is likely to give, with a line ready for it.

4 · Flows

What comes in, what goes out

Flows in
  • account + what’s happening from Signal Console
  • who to call (the person + their role)
Flows out
  • on a booked meeting → creates a Deal (into Deal Workspace)
  • call outcomes → Dashboard · Deal Workspace · Readiness · Handoff Kit
5 · Language — plain, mid-sales-legible

The user is the seller

Banned on the face: thread · loom · pull · pressure · proof · say-next · call memory · “the move.” The user is the seller (or you), never “rep.”
6 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • The deal-creation on a booked meeting
  • The prep-then-capture shape
  • The live consequence of a real call
What it is NOT
  • Never a script library
  • Never a during-call clicker
  • Not a live-call console
  • Not a dialer
