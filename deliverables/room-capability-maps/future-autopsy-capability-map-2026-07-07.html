1 · Purpose

What the room is for

Diagnose failure before it happens. Forensic light-table posture: each at-risk deal is pinned as a case, its causal pattern named, its corrective route made obvious. Never soften into “risk review” — this is consequential route correction, not commentary.

5 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

What it is NOT
