Signal Console · tool used during live work · capability map

Monitor account signals and priority.

Type of area: a tool used while the work is happening · protected product area · settled 2026-07-04 (Attention Router)

Signal Console stores watched accounts and supporting signals, then ranks them using signal type, credibility, and age. Review those inputs before recommending outreach.

Sourced from: canon §4.7 + the account-priority engine (shipped src/signal-console) + ADR-006 (substrate role). Settled design: signal-console-attention-router-2026-07-04.html.
1 · Purpose

What this area is for

Explain account priority using current signals and recorded activity. Preserve the ranking math and data contracts. Research is useful when it supports a specific account decision; do not infer buyer intent or urgency merely from a score.
2 · The account-priority engine

How an account ranks

Priority is computed, not stored — the source of truth is the signals + their timestamps.
  • Priority score = signal count × type weight × source credibility × how recent each signal is
  • Bands (the Attention Router): High priority · Priority declining · New signal or newly watched · Monitor — priority is shown as a band, not a bare number

4 · Flows

What comes in, what goes out

Flows in
  • accounts from Prospecting Desk + Territory Architect
  • ICP-Match score from ICP Studio
Flows out
  • ranked accounts → Outbound Studio · Cold Call Studio · LinkedIn Playbook
  • account priority + available evidence → Dashboard
  • account priority + activity state → sales handoff documentation
  • accounts + signals → the Briefing (read-only data source per ADR-006)
  • act-on handoffs → Outbound · Discovery Studio · Cold Call · Deal Workspace
5 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • Signal interpretation
  • Connect account research to a concrete outreach or monitoring decision
  • Account priority meaning
  • A protected product area — may not be made generic, renamed, or folded in
What it is NOT
  • Never a badge list
  • Never a passive timeline
  • Not a notification feed
  • Not a static account directory
