Readiness Score · System Ledger · capability map

Could a real first-hire walk in tomorrow and run the motion?

System Ledger (synthesis) · design settled 2026-07-07 (the climb)

Answer one question — is the system inheritable yet? — and give the operator a verdict, not a number. Readiness is a maturity assessment, not a pressure rail: an integral over time, not a derivative. The Dashboard already answers “what should I act on today?”; Future Autopsy answers “what’s decaying?”; Readiness answers “is the workspace inheritable yet?”

Sourced from: canon §4.17 + the readiness engine (verdict.ts · dimensions.ts · dashboard readiness-aggregator). Settled design: readiness-score-climb-2026-07-07.html.
1 · Purpose

What the room is for

Give the operator a single truth about inheritability, prominent but never obnoxious — anchored on the Dashboard topbar at all times. The room shows the verdict, the gating evidence behind it, and what would change the verdict next. Internal dimension scoring stays as the math; the operator never sees a “/20” or the word “dimension.”
2 · The five verdicts

Gate-based, not threshold-based

Verdicts are present-evidence gates, not score thresholds. Each gate uses the internal 5-part × 20 math but the face names the state, never a number.
VerdictWhat it meansGate (internal)
You are the systemFounder-led, no transferable surface yetany part at 0

Hire-ready, repeatableMultiple wins, losses analyzed, advisors lit, kit composedall Hire-ready + win/loss ≥1 AND advisors ≥1 AND handoff ≥5/7 ready

4 · Flows

What comes in, what goes out

Flows in
  • summaries from every deep room (cloud-synced via data-client.ts, not the legacy snapshot key)
  • deal-health vitals from Deal Workspace
  • Pilot Desk outcomes
  • Future Autopsy run history
  • advisor deployment outcomes from Call in a Favor
  • section-readiness count from Founding GTM (drives the repeatable gate)
Flows out
  • verdict + topbar anchor into the Dashboard
  • a verdict transition UP fires the ceremony moment in Founding GTM
  • verdict + gating evidence into the Welcome read

6 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • Verdict as gate-based truth (not threshold-based)
  • The singular question (hire-ready or not)
  • The ceremony at verdict transitions UP
  • The climb — readiness felt as an integral over time
What it is NOT
  • Never duplicate what the Dashboard says about “what’s weakest now”
  • Never a 5-bar score breakdown as the primary surface
  • Not a pressure rail
  • Bars are decoration — the verdict is the value
