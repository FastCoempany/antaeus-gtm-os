Onboarding collects the initial target, account, deal, and quota information used by the workspace. Explain required inputs, optional inputs, and which information may be enriched.

1 · Purpose

What the room is for

Ask for the seller’s judgment where automated enrichment cannot supply it. Describe setup progress and generated assessments explicitly. Preserve required-field validation and the ability to return to unfinished setup.
2 · The seven-step arc

The settled onboarding data collection

A fixed, ordered flow. The heavy middle (live deals) is the judgment no automation can supply; everything the app can enrich, it enriches.
#StepWhat it asks / does
1DoorwayLeads with the honesty — “this asks real work of you, here’s the deal.” The honest pitch, not an apology.
2ICPInterview-style — the app pushes back on a vague answer until it’s a target it can hunt.
3AccountsTakes names only and enriches them with available triggers, firmographics, and account-priority inputs.
4Account researchAntaeus searches the web for recent, source-backed updates on each named account and ranks the list by recency and relevance to the ICP (an assessment, not buying intent).
5Live dealsThe heavy middle — the operator fills the judgment only they hold (champion, who signs, where it’s stuck); the company came enriched. The deal count climbs toward the minimum of ten.
6QuotaThe number set — seeds Quota Workback + the outbound seed.
7LandingConfirms setup, suggests where to start (an account and a recommended action) based on what was entered, lists what can be added later, and opens the Dashboard.
3 · The tier model

Gate the load-bearing, invite the rest

Heavy-seeding doctrine #6. The deal-level judgment is the hard floor — the thing that stops Antaeus being a prettier CRM.
TierWhatRule
RequiredICP + the account list + ≥10 live dealsFewer than ten is allowed; explain that the analysis has fewer deal examples.
Invitedpilot evidence · advisors · win/loss historyPost-landing, payoff-anchored prompts gathered over days — never walled.
4 · What it offers

The primitives

Capability / surfaceWhat it does
The doorwayThe honest pitch — frames the friction as the sign they’re doing the rare thing.
The interview-style ICP questionsPushes back on a vague answer until the ICP is a target the system can hunt.
Name-only account capture → enrichmentThe operator supplies account names; enrichment returns available signals, firmographics, and account-priority inputs.
The live-deal judgment cardschampion / who signs / where it’s stuck / budget-timeline — the only-they-know judgment; the company arrives enriched.
The ≥10-deal floorRequired seller judgment, with an explicit limited-sample explanation when fewer deals are available.
The quota setThe number, worked into the daily habit downstream.
The landing hand-offSuggests where to start from what was entered, then opens the Dashboard.
The persistent evidence marginA recessive, blue “why we ask” note on every ask that opens to a real, citable source — never assertion.
5 · Flows

What comes in, what goes out

Onboarding does not store to a separate “completion” record — its output becomes the live rooms.
Flows in
  • new-user context (company, role, product category)
Flows out
  • gtmos_icp_analytics — the specific ICP from the interview (→ ICP Studio)
  • gtmos_sc_v4 + gtmos_signal_room_health — accounts (→ Signal Console)
  • gtmos_deal_workspaces — the live deals (→ Deal Workspace)
  • gtmos_qw_inputs + gtmos_outbound_seed — quota (→ Quota Workback)
  • gtmos_activation_context + gtmos_onboarding — completion; all cloud-mirrored via ADR-007. Hands off to Welcome.
6 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • Heavy-seeding — never re-soften into a low-friction form that apologizes for the work
  • Onboarding output becoming the live rooms (never a separate “completion” screen)
  • The ≥10-deal judgment floor
  • The citable evidence margin
  • “They bring judgment, the app brings depth”
What it is NOT
  • Not a low-friction sign-up form
  • Not a CRM data-entry grid
  • Not a wizard that hides the work
  • Not a backstage record the operator never returns to
