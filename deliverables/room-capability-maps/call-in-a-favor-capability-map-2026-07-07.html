Call in a Favor · Live Instrument · capability map

Mobilize the people in your corner to move a stuck deal.

Live Instrument (live execution) · renamed from Advisor Deploy 2026-07-06 · settled (the relay)

Mobilize the people in your corner — investors, advisors, happy customers — to move a stuck deal you can’t move alone. Prepare one backchannel ask before you spend that trust, route it, log it, watch it come back as deal movement. A founder’s real unfair advantage is their network; this room makes that deliberate instead of ad-hoc. Deal-first: the room opens on the stuck deals that need outside help.

Sourced from: canon §4.16 (renamed + deal-first + plain-language teardown, 2026-07-06) + shipped src/advisor-deploy. Settled design: call-in-a-favor-toprail-2026-07-06.html.
1 · Purpose

What the room is for

Know who you can call on, match the right person to the right deal, pick the right ask for the deal’s state, write the exact message AND the note they forward to the buyer — don’t spend trust on a vague ask, don’t over-tap the same person, and close every loop. The favor is a relay: you → your person → the buyer.
2 · The favor as a hand-off

You → your person → the buyer

The room writes both halves. The discipline is making the person’s half easy to say yes to — the easier their lift, the more likely they help, and the less you lean on a favor you may need again.

3 · The ten asks

What you’re asking for

Ten plain situations, matched to the deal’s state.
  • open the door · get an exec involved · nudge a stalled pilot · unstick procurement · reset the comparison · lost your champion · save it from a budget freeze · signal you’re a serious bet · get a customer to vouch · warm up a renewal

7 · Guardrails

Must never be flattened · what it is NOT

Must never be flattened
  • The deal-linked consequence (a favor is not a side log — it moves a specific deal)
  • The don’t-waste-trust discipline (ready check + don’t-over-ask)
  • The hand-off craft (both notes, easy to say yes to)
  • Closing the loop
What it is NOT
  • Not a side log
  • Not a contact CRM
  • Not a mass-intro tool
  • Not a networking rolodex
