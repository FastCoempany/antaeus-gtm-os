# AE Hiring Brief, premium page: copy rewrite

**What this is:** the uploaded `should-we-hire-the-next-ae-premium.html` (the $500 / $1,000 / $7,500–$15,000 version) with its reader-facing words scrubbed to the same standard as the product page and the $149 AE page: `COPY_STANDARD.md` plus canon voice rules §11/§13. Only the words people read changed (69 swaps). Markup, styles, fonts and the brand mark are untouched.

This page was already much plainer than the $149 version. Most of the work was removing a handful of insider words that recur throughout:

- **"motion"** → *sales process* (the hero, the cost section, the founder letter, the "Lifted / Grounded" section, the outcomes).
- **"carry" / "feed" / "seat"** → *support*, *enough pipeline for*, *role* or *hire*.
- **"The method"** used as a product name → *the analysis*.
- **Sample states:** *Conditional* → *Supported with conditions*, *Thin* → *Short*. *Demand supply* → *Pipeline supply*, *Motion repeatability* → *Repeatable without the founder*. The four outcomes now read *Supported / Supported with conditions / Not yet supported / Not enough information yet*.
- **"Pipeline supply is the limiting condition"** → *There isn't enough qualified pipeline yet for another seller* (in the sample and where the brief quotes it).
- **The 18-month clock metaphor.** The hero now says *Most sales leaders get about 18 months to show results. The next AE they hire often decides how those months go.* The timeline labels are literal (*You get the role approved and recruit*, *They're expected to hit quota*).
- **Founder letter.** It keeps its voice and every claim. Only the insider words changed: "does the motion survive the person running it?" → *does the sales process still work when the person who built it isn't the one selling?*
- **Accuracy fix.** The hero said the reviewer "has watched this hire go wrong five times"; the letter says five *companies*. The hero now matches the letter.

## Checks

- Render at 1440 / 820 / 390 / 360 / 320: no sideways scroll, no clipped text and no page errors, before or after.
- The headline runs one or two lines longer on narrow screens (390: 6→7, 320: 8→10) because the first sentence gained "to show results".
- The jargon scan finds no *motion*, *carry*, *seat*, *method*, *thin* or *clock* left in reader-facing text. The only remaining hits are "the math", which is used literally.

## Not changed, worth a look

- **"$200–300k" for a wrong hire, and "about 18 months" for a sales leader's tenure.** Both are stated without a source. They read as the founder's experience, which is fine in the letter but stronger as a headline. Consider citing a source or wording them as the founder's own observation.
- **"Lifted" / "Grounded."** These are the brand's Living Mark device (canon Part II §3), so they stay. The headings and copy under each one now say plainly what they mean.
- **Kept as-is:** the draft banner, the prices, the tier structure, the FAQ answers apart from "method" and "seat", and the "The one I'd pick" note.
