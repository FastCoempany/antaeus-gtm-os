Before you start

This setup needs real thought from you.

You'll enter what only you know: who you sell to, which companies you're going after, and where each of your open deals stands. For every deal, that's who's championing it, who signs, and what's holding it up. Without that, the first sign one of your deals is in trouble is going to be an email saying they went with someone else.

You enter this once. Every morning after, your Dashboard opens on what needs you first, and why. Your open deals are lined up behind it in order of risk, so the ones that have gone quiet or have no next step booked come first. Add the deals you're unsure about too. They'll show up there with the rest.
