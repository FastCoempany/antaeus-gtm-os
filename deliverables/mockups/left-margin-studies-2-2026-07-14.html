LM-8 · The Lamp. The margin reads with you: as your cursor moves down the room, a warm beam tracks the row under your eye — the row glows faintly from the left, and the margin whispers that row's one move at the foot of the page. Nothing to learn, nothing to click (clicking the row still does what it always did) — the left edge simply pays attention to the same line you are.

LM-9 · The Seismograph. The left margin is the last seven days of your workspace, drawn as one ink trace — time runs down, today at the top with a slow pulse. Quiet days run flat; the needle kicks where something happened — red kicks left for decay, forest kicks right for wins, blue for signals. Hover a kick and the margin tells you what it was. The room's memory, living where your eye lands first.
