# Creative Brief · D2 — THE MYTH
*Strength from the ground. The brand's origin story as the film.*

**Runtime:** 70s · **Aspect:** 1920×1080 · **Status:** brief — build-ready
**Register:** mythic, slow, weighty. The viewer shouldn't be sure it's a
software film until 0:40 — and should be certain it's a great one by 0:20.

---

## 1 · The stage

- **Space:** the product's own bright field, `#f6f8fc`, with the faint
  graph-paper undertexture from the design system at 40% of its usual
  presence (the world before the system: paper, but empty). Air, not walls.
- **The horizon:** a hairline at 78% frame height (low third), `#0a1c40` at
  12% opacity in Act I — barely there. It is the film's other protagonist.
- **The mark:** the Grounded-A at spec geometry (48-viewBox paths), rendered
  as strokes only. Its condition is the story:
  - *Lifted:* stroke-width 2.2 (vs spec 3.4), opacity 55%, tilted −7°,
    floating at 22% frame height. The crossbar is MISSING (hollow — the gauge
    reads nothing).
  - *Grounded:* stroke 3.4, opacity 100%, level, feet exactly on the line,
    crossbar present.
- **Light:** Act I is over-lit and empty (bright reads as exposed, not
  cheerful). From TOUCH onward, a soft warm cast rises from below the line
  (`rgba(230,112,30,.04)` gradient, bottom 20% of frame) — the ground is
  where the warmth is.
- **Camera:** locked. The myth doesn't need a camera; it needs gravity.
  The only camera move in the film is a slow 4% push-in across Act III.

## 2 · Type system

| Role | Face | Size | Color |
|---|---|---|---|
| The truths (Act I) | DM Serif Display | 54px | `#0a1c40` |
| The single word beats ("Yours.") | DM Serif Display | 96px | `#0a1c40` |
| Ground whispers | JetBrains Mono 700, +0.2em, uppercase | 13px | `rgba(10,28,64,.34)` |
| Grown-object labels (Act III) | mixed per shard, always smaller than the myth type | — | semantic roles |
| Closing line | DM Serif Display italic | 44px | `#0a1c40` |

**Color law:** Act I is ink-only. The first non-ink color in the film is the
contact pulse (a single orange ring, once). Act III uses the semantic roles
sparingly (one red truth, one orange answer, one forest fill). Act IV returns
to ink + one forest accent on the second mark (durable-over-time — the hire
is the integral).

## 3 · Motion vocabulary

- **Sinking:** the mark loses 6–8% frame height after each Act I truth —
  eased over the full text hold, imperceptible in the moment, undeniable in
  sum.
- **The contact pulse:** one orange ring expanding from the feet (240ms out,
  400ms fade), then the line wakes: weight animates 1px→2.5px as it runs to
  both edges (900ms, settle ease).
- **Growth:** everything in Act III enters ONLY by rising from the line —
  translateY from below with a slight overshoot-and-settle (the Ground
  summon's own motion). Nothing fades in mid-air. This is the film's layout
  law and its thesis.
- **Recession:** grown objects return INTO the line (never fade out) —
  reversed growth, faster.
- **The second mark:** rises from *beneath* the line (implying the ground
  held it all along), settles feet-first.

## 4 · The script (final)

**ACT I — LIFTED**
1. *Every deal your company has ever won lives in one head.*
2. *Every reason. Every relationship. Every save.*
3. **Yours.** *(single word, 96px)*
4. whisper, as the mark reaches its lowest drift: `AND HEADS DON'T SCALE`

**ACT II — TOUCH**
*(no words — the pulse, the line waking, then:)*
5. whisper, rippling along the line: `WATCHING · COUNTING · REMEMBERING`

**ACT III — RISE** *(each line arrives with its grown shard)*
6. *It reads the world for you.* — grown: a rising stem of drifting account
   names; `BOEING` ignites; mono under it: `A REAL EVENT · A REAL REASON ·
   TODAY`
7. *It counts the work — even the work you don't log.* — grown: the count
   stem, digits rolling `13 → 14`
8. *It says the hard thing out loud.* — grown, red serif: **3 deals will
   slip this week if you do nothing.** — beat — orange answer rises beside
   it: **Here's the move.**
9. *It answers before you're asked.* — grown: the coverage bar filling
   118 of 150; mono: `THE PAPERS YOU ALREADY HOLD`
10. *And it writes everything down — for the one who comes next.* — grown:
    the open book rising to rest ON the line

**ACT IV — STAND**
11. *(the grown things recede into the line; the mark stands full-stroke)*
12. *Built so the next person can stand on it.* — as the second, smaller
    mark rises and lands beside the first (forest-tinted stroke)
13. End card: `ANTAEUS` · **Strength, from the ground.** · `antaeus.app`

## 6 · Sound

- Act I: near-silence — thin air tone, a faint heartbeat that slows as the
  mark sinks (weakening).
- The touch: ONE deep, dry impact (felt, not boomy) — then the heartbeat
  returns, steady and stronger, and stays under everything as the film's
  pulse.
- Act III: each growth gets a low woody rise; the red truth mutes everything
  for its beat; the orange answer restores it.
- Act IV: the second mark's landing is a soft double of the touch impact.
  End card in silence.

## 7 · Cut-downs

The film resists fragment cuts (the arc IS the asset). Two sanctioned shards:
(a) 0:15–0:24 "Yours. → TOUCH" as a 10s brand sting; (b) 0:37–0:45 the
red-truth/orange-answer pivot as the product-severity single.

## 8 · Production notes

- Stage `stage-myth.html`; the mark's two states are the same SVG paths with
  animated stroke-width/opacity/rotation — never two assets.
- The Act I sink must be verified against a ruler overlay in test frames
  (drift too fast = comic, too slow = invisible; target 7% height per truth).
- Filmed in 4 scenes (per act); the push-in is CSS transform on the stage
  root, not the camera.

## 9 · Acceptance checks

- The layout law holds: nothing in Acts II–IV enters except from the line.
- The mark's geometry matches spec 10 at both states (overlay-diff a frame
  against `public/favicon.svg` paths).
- Act I reads as NOT-software; Act III reads as unmistakably-this-software.
- Voice gate on all strings; one-frame test; mute test.
