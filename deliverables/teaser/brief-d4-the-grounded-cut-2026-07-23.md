# Creative Brief · D4 — THE GROUNDED CUT
*The Myth as the frame. The Shards as the language. The Day's clock as the pulse.*

**Runtime:** 75s · **Aspect:** 1920×1080 · **Status:** brief — build-ready
**Register:** meaning → spectacle → promise, in that order. The flagship cut.

---

## 1 · The stage

- **Space:** the bright field throughout — D4 never goes to the dark theater
  (that's D1's move). Graph-paper undertexture at 40% in Act I, rising to
  full design-system presence once the ground wakes (the world becoming the
  product's world).
- **The ground line:** D2's horizon, same behavior (12% ghost in Act I; wakes
  at TOUCH; carries everything after). After TOUCH it is never off-screen.
- **The mark:** D2's two states, exactly (hollow/lifted → full/grounded).
- **The clock:** D3's mono corner clock — but it DOES NOT EXIST until the
  moment of contact. Time starts when the ground wakes. It dies again at the
  end card, replaced by `6:04 AM TOMORROW`.
- **Field temperature:** borrowed from D3 but compressed — cool at 6:04,
  true-bright by mid-morning beats, warm slant by the evening beat, never
  reaching D3's night-navy (the film ends at dusk's edge, not midnight —
  the myth's close needs the bright field).
- **Camera:** locked in Acts I/II/IV. One slow 5% push-in across Act III
  (the only move, inherited from D2).

## 2 · The three laws (what makes this cut ours alone)

1. **The growth law:** from TOUCH onward, nothing enters frame except by
   rising from the ground line. No fades-in-place, no slides from the edges
   (the one sanctioned exception: the face-off columns in beat 6:22 slide
   horizontally ALONG the line — they still originate from it).
2. **The honest clock:** the corner clock jumps like a real day — clustered
   morning, stretched afternoon. It never advances during Acts I/II/IV.
3. **The one orange frame:** full-orange treatment happens exactly once —
   the 10:48 reply. Every other use of orange is a thin rule or a single
   word. (Red once, at 4:45. Forest twice: the coverage fill and the second
   mark.)

## 3 · Type system

Inherits D2's myth type for Acts I/II/IV and D1's shard type for Act III,
with one reconciliation: Act III headlines cap at 64px so the myth's Act I
truths (54px) and the single-word "Yours." (96px) remain the film's largest
text until the closing line. Hierarchy = the myth outranks the machine.

## 4 · The script (final)

**ACT I — LIFTED (no clock)**
1. *Your whole revenue motion lives in one head.*
2. **Yours.**
   *(the hollow mark sinks under each; whisper as it bottoms out:
   `AND HEADS DON'T SCALE`)*

**ACT II — TOUCH**
3. *(stillness — contact — the pulse; the line wakes edge to edge)*
   whisper along the line: `WATCHING · COUNTING · REMEMBERING`
   → the clock ignites: `6:04 AM`

**ACT III — THE DAY RISES (shards grown from the line, clocked)**
4. `7:31 AM` — **The Pick.** Dim roster names rise like heat off the line;
   `BOEING` ignites (thin-orange word, not the full frame). Serif: *The one
   move that matters. Picked. With the reason attached.* Mono: `HEAT 62 ·
   FRESH SIGNALS`
5. `9:12 AM` — **The Wire.** Tagged sentences stack upward; the count rolls
   `13 → 14` at the stem's top. Serif: *Captured. Counted. Nobody logged
   anything.*
6. `10:48 AM` — **The Ignition.** The film's one full-orange frame:
   **Starbucks replied — after 20 quiet days.** tag `THE BUYERS`. Everything
   else on screen dims 20% for its 6 seconds.
7. `2:20 PM` — **The Thread.** `BOEING` again, pale; the line draws through
   five waypoints: `THE SIGNAL · THE MESSAGE · THE CALL · THE DEAL · THE
   FAVOR`. Serif: *One account. Its whole life. One thread.*
8. `4:45 PM` — **The Honest Beat.** Red, alone: **3 deals will slip this
   week if you do nothing.** Beat. A thin-orange answer rises beside it:
   **Here's the move.**
9. `6:22 PM` — **The Face-off.** THEIR SIDE — *150 security questions before
   anything moves.* / YOUR LINE — *Your papers already answer 118.* Forest
   bar fills between them along the ground line itself.
10. `11:47 PM` — **The Settling.** Everything Act III grew loosens and
    sediments down the stems into an open book resting ON the line. Serif:
    *Everything compounds into what your first hire opens on day one.*

**ACT IV — STAND (clock gone)**
11. *(the book closes; the line quiet; the mark full-stroke, planted —
    two seconds of stillness)*
12. The second, smaller mark rises from beneath the line, lands feet-first
    beside the first (forest-tinted stroke). Serif italic: *Built so the
    next person can stand on it.*
13. End card: `ANTAEUS` · **Strength, from the ground.** · `antaeus.app` ·
    tiny mono beneath: `6:04 AM TOMORROW`

## 6 · Sound

D2's architecture with D3's accents: near-silence and a slowing heartbeat in
Act I; ONE dry impact at contact, after which the heartbeat locks steady as
the film's pulse; each Act III growth gets its low woody rise, digit rolls
tick, 10:48 gets the warm swell, 4:45 mutes to heartbeat-only for its beat;
the second mark's landing softly doubles the contact impact; card in silence
with one final soft intake breath under `6:04 AM TOMORROW`.

## 7 · Cut-downs

- **The 15s sting:** shots 02–03 (Yours. → TOUCH → clock ignition). The
  brand in one breath.
- **Hour shards:** each Act III beat recomposes as a 7–9s vertical (clock
  top, shard center, ground line at the bottom edge — the line is the
  vertical crops' constant).
- **The 30s festival cut:** 02–03 + 06 + 08 + 12. Meaning, spectacle,
  severity, promise.

## 8 · Production notes

- Stage `stage-grounded.html`. Acts share one DOM: the line, mark, and clock
  are persistent elements; Act III shards are templated growth components
  keyed `?beat=`. Shot per-scene on the proven rig; the push-in is a root
  transform.
- Reuses D2's mark rig and D3's clock/temperature rigs verbatim — build D2
  and D3 stages first if all four shoot; D4 assembles from their parts plus
  D1's shard components. (This is the cheapest-to-build of the four IF the
  others exist; the most expensive if built alone.)
- Sequence check before shooting: the growth law is enforceable in review by
  scrubbing any entrance frame-by-frame — an element whose first visible
  pixel is not at the line fails the shot.

## 9 · Acceptance checks

- The three laws hold (growth law scrub; clock-jump audit; count the color
  frames: one full orange, one red, two forest).
- Act I contains no product nouns; Act III contains no myth type at >64px;
  the clock exists only between TOUCH and the settling.
- The mark geometry matches spec 10 in both states; the second mark is 82%
  scale of the first.
- One-frame, mute, and voice-gate tests as standard.
