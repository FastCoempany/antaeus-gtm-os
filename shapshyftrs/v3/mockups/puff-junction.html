About

Four pieces of cast concrete and machined brass, stacked. The lid is knurled so it turns with one hand, and the mark on top is pressed two tenths of a millimetre into the metal — you find it with your thumb before you find it with your eyes.

The sides are ours: pop-art panels printed edge to edge, so the thing reads as an object on a shelf long before it reads as a grinder.

Specs

  • 85 × 85 mm square body, 57 mm tall
  • Ø63 mm brass cylinder, standing 15 mm proud
  • Eleven parts — two magnets, threaded lid, mesh and holder
  • Diamond knurl on the turning band, 1.26 mm pitch
  • Cast concrete, machined brass, neodymium magnets
